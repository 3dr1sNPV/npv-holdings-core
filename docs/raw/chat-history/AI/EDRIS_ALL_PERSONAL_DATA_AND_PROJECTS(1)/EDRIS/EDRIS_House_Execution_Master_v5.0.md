5. LE products may be more expressive, but may not violate house DNA.
6. Future products remain outside launch until they pass the same audit.

**Important:** these raw measurements must be reconciled against the tailor's measurement chart before cutting. They are not automatically finished-garment measurements.

## Lapel engineering
The lapel must read as an architectural line, not a soft curve.

Rules:
- 12 cm finished width at the widest controlled point.
- Straight outer edge from break toward peak.
- Straight/controlled inner edge.
- Peak tip sharp and symmetrical.
- No decorative belly.
- No exaggerated roll that visually curves the line.
- Gorge and break must be aligned to preserve the long Hollywood proportion.
- Satin facing follows the architectural geometry cleanly.

## Jacket construction order
1. Verify paper pattern.
2. Cut shell, canvas, chest piece and lining.
3. Mark grain, balance lines and pocket placements.
4. Build front canvas and chest.
5. Shape shoulder and chest by hand pressing.
6. Form lapel roll without creating a visibly curved outer edge.
7. Construct collar and gorge.
8. Set sleeves at high armhole with controlled pitch.
9. Construct double-jetted pockets.
10. Construct double vents.
11. Close body and lining.
12. Hand finish buttonhole and button.
13. Apply aged-silver hardware.
14. Final press.
15. Fit on body.
16. Record alterations.
17. Produce golden sample record.

## 001 QC gate
- Lapels: 12 cm ± approved production tolerance.
- Left/right peak symmetry.
- Break height symmetry.
- Shoulder balance.
- Collar clean against neck.
- Sleeve pitch natural at rest.
- No pulling across chest.
- No collapse at armhole.
- Pocket jets parallel.
- Vents equal.
- Trouser crease centered.
- Satin stripe uninterrupted.
- Shirt studs aligned.
- Silver hardware finish consistent.
- White shirt remains deliberately crisp against black tuxedo.

# 7. VISUAL DESIGN SYSTEM

## 7.1 Wordmark
EDRIS wordmark must be restrained, architectural and legible. No decorative Persian imitation. No permanent dependency on the crest.

## 7.2 Masked Rabbit
Use as a house signature, not wallpaper.

Primary forms:
- blind/debossed
- cast metal
- engraved
- embroidered only when materially justified
- architectural negative space

Never use as a cartoon mascot.

## 7.3 Typography
System should use:
- one high-contrast editorial serif or equivalent for ceremonial/editorial expression
- one neutral architectural sans for navigation, product data and utility
- one optional monospaced/technical face for provenance and atelier records

Typography must feel cinematic and engineered rather than futuristic.

## 7.4 Photography
Six recurring visual worlds:
- ceremony
- architecture
- weather
- wilderness
- machine/workshop
- archive/memory

Rules:
- human presence over sterile product-only imagery
- controlled shadow
- tactile material detail
- no excessive black crush
- no generic Persian props
- no fake aristocratic mansion clichés
- no AI-generated people presented as real campaigns

## 7.5 Color architecture
Core: black, forest-black, ivory/white, mineral grey, aged silver.
Secondary: bottle green, black-burgundy, mineral brown.
No rainbow seasonal identity.

## 7.6 Hardware
Master geometry:
**narrow vertical incision crossing a framed/stepped architectural form.**

The geometry should be recognizable across a button, clasp, ring, watch crown and fragrance cap without literally repeating the same object.

# 15. COSTING / PRICING WORKFLOW

For every product:

**MATERIAL COST + COMPONENT COST + LABOR + DEVELOPMENT AMORTIZATION + PACKAGING + LOGISTICS + QC + OVERHEAD ALLOCATION = LANDED COST**

Then establish target gross margin and channel economics.

Pricing must reflect:
- craft
- scarcity
- material quality
- development complexity
- aftercare
- provenance
- brand position

Never price solely from competitor imitation.

# 16. QC MASTER GATE

A product fails release if any of the following is unresolved:

- unsafe construction
- unacceptable material performance
- dimensional instability
- inconsistent hardware finish
- poor fit
- weak closure/fastener performance
- packaging damage risk
- missing care instructions
- incomplete provenance
- incorrect labeling
- unresolved supplier quality
- unresolved regulatory requirement
- unacceptable visual deviation from golden sample

Golden sample is the visual and construction reference.

# 17. HOUSE-LEVEL FINAL CHECK

Before any collection launch ask:

### Identity
Does it feel unmistakably EDRIS without the logo?

### Function
Does it solve a real problem?

### Beauty
Is it beautiful before the story is explained?

### Strangeness
Is there a second-glance detail?

### Meaning
Does the object carry a deeper house idea?

### Iranian soul
Is Iran present in the thinking rather than costume?

### Longevity
Would it still make sense in twenty years?

### System
Does it connect naturally to another EDRIS object?

### Craft
Can it be made properly?

### Archive
Will it become more interesting with age?

If the answer is no, redesign or delete.
