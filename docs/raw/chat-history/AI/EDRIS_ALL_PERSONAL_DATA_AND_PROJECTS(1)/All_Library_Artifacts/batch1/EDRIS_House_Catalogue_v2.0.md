# 3. 2026 TREND TRANSLATION — NOT TREND CHASING

## Current signals adopted

### A. Slimmer, more controlled menswear
FW26 reporting points toward slimmer tops, fitted waists and more controlled outerwear after years of oversized silhouettes. citeturn0search1

**EDRIS translation:** sculpted rather than skinny; strong shoulder + controlled waist + clean vertical line. Keep 001's long Hollywood body and 12 cm straight lapel. Expand fitted house blocks for shirts, knitwear and selected coats.

### B. Formality is becoming fluid
2026 menswear increasingly mixes tuxedo/formal codes with denim, loosened ties and casual elements. citeturn0search0

**EDRIS translation:** build a wardrobe where the same house language moves from tuxedo → tailored suit → knit → denim → technical outerwear without losing ceremony.

### C. Neck accessories, brooches and tie hardware
Neckties, collars, brooches and tie pins are rising as expressive menswear details. citeturn0search1turn0search6

**EDRIS translation:** make the hardware family important: aged-silver tie bars, collar bars, lapel pins and a signature EDRIS brooch derived from the Masked Rabbit geometry.

### D. Rugged luxury / sophisticated utility
2026 forecasts highlight refined utility, durable materials, field jackets, earth tones and outdoor-performance references. citeturn0search1

**EDRIS translation:** add an EDRIS field/utility capsule in forest, tobacco-black, mineral brown and oxidized olive, using tailoring-level construction rather than gorpcore or military cosplay.

### E. Craftsmanship and investment value
Luxury's recalibration is moving attention back toward creativity, quality, craftsmanship and meaningful product value. Jewelry is a particularly strong category, while resale/provenance is increasingly important. citeturn0search2

**EDRIS translation:** jewelry, hardware, repairability, provenance, archive and aftercare become central house systems, not extras.

### F. Smart eyewear + AI discovery
Smart frames are expected to become a major wearable format, while AI increasingly mediates fashion search and product discovery. citeturn0search2turn0search4

**EDRIS translation:** build EDRIS eyewear with an eventual smart-frame architecture, but preserve the visual identity. Make the website/product catalogue semantically rich and machine-readable so AI systems can understand every product, material, size, story and availability.

### G. Watch challenge
Traditional watch usage is under pressure from smartwatches, especially among younger consumers. citeturn0news9

**EDRIS translation:** do not make a generic luxury watch. Make an exceptionally legible tactical-dress mechanical watch that earns wrist time through utility, Bond versatility, craftsmanship and provenance.

# 13. HOROLOGY — THE TACTICAL DRESS WATCH PROGRAM

## DESIGN BRIEF FROM FOUNDER TASTE
The founder's reference set is **Seiko SKX007 + Rolex Submariner + Vacheron Constantin + James Bond**.

Translation:
- SKX007 = honest instrument, legibility, durability, tool-watch character.
- Submariner = iconic dive-watch discipline, black dial/bezel, robust proportions.
- Vacheron Constantin = finishing, refinement, heritage and mechanical beauty.
- Bond = tactical elegance; a watch that can live with a suit and with a field jacket.

EDRIS must not copy any reference. It should synthesize the principles into an original house object.

## EDRIS 001 — THE OPERATIVE
**Core watch.**
- 40–41 mm case.
- Slim enough for a tuxedo cuff.
- Automatic mechanical movement.
- Strong water resistance.
- Screw-down crown.
- Rotating dive-style bezel.
- Matte black dial.
- Restrained aged-silver/gunmetal indices.
- Sword/architectural hands.
- Strong lume.
- Date only if it improves utility.
- EDRIS at 12; AUTOMATIC or equivalent restrained technical text at 6.
- Brushed bracelet with polished architectural bevels and clean taper.
- Black leather, refined rubber and black NATO options.
- Caseback: small Masked Rabbit maker seal.
- Movement: excellent finishing, appropriate to price tier; decorated rotor with restrained EDRIS geometry.

**Positioning:** a tactical instrument dressed for civilization.

## EDRIS 002 — THE HOUSE
- 39–40 mm.
- Thin refined case.
- No dive bezel.
- Black or ivory dial.
- Precious-metal option.
- Hand-finished or high-grade decorated movement.
- Architectural indices.
- Slim black leather strap.
- Tuxedo/black-tie use.

## EDRIS 003 — THE FIELD
Future rugged watch: slightly tougher, black/forest dial, textile or bracelet options, field-inspired but unmistakably luxury.

## DIGITAL WATCH PASSPORT
Every EDRIS watch should have a digital identity tied to serial number, movement, service history, authenticity and ownership/provenance with privacy controls.

# 34. MASTER HOUSE STATEMENT

EDRIS explores the tension between civilization and the feral human through clothing, objects, fragrance, jewelry, eyewear, horology and architecture. Iranian soul is hidden within an international language of Art Deco geometry, Gothic monumentality, cinematic glamour, engineered craft and controlled darkness.

We are born feral.
We build civilization around ourselves.
We make beauty, machines, architecture and ritual to give meaning to existence.
But civilization decays.
The body returns to nature.
The object becomes an artifact.
The memory becomes a story.
The story becomes legacy.

**We civilize the wild. We build against time. We know everything eventually returns to nature. So we make something worth leaving behind.**
