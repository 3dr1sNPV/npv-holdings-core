# House EDRIS & Tux Gen 001 — Design System Summary

Design Ethos

House EDRIS is a luxury fashion/artifact brand defined by "dark architecture": a synthesis of ancient Iranian aesthetics, cinematic glamour, and tactical, engineered tailoring. The design language prioritizes permanence, structural precision, and strong monochrome palettes.

Tux Gen 001 — Key Attributes

- Silhouette: Architectural, vertical emphasis, structured shoulder (authoritative yet unpadded), single-breasted with extended overlap (deep V) and low single-button closure or hidden interior anchor.
- Lapel: Peak (spear) lapel recommended at 12 cm (approx. 4.7") for a bold statement; requires a high gorge line and balanced shoulder width to avoid overwhelm.
- Fabric & Finish: Heavy-weight midnight-black or forest-black ultra-matte wool that absorbs light; textured oxidized-gunmetal/aged-silver accents instead of satin.
- Lining & Trim: Bottle-green or deep-burgundy silk lining; subtle hardware in aged silver and oxidized metals.
- Construction Notes: Emphasize clean bellied peak roll, elongated lapel throat, and a slightly extended front panel for the overlap; internal structure to support the 12 cm lapel without distortion.

Brand Elements

- Masked Rabbit Crest: A composite of sacred geometry (HoZho), mechanical grill elements (Mick Thomson style), Corvo/Dark Souls monumentality, and Art Deco retro-futurist linework.
- Olfactory Pairing: Dark, resinous fragrances like Nasomato Black Afgano or Dior Homme Parfum recommended as signature scents.

Tailor's Spec Recommendations

1. Lock lapel width at 12 cm only after testing on muslin or mockup with proposed shoulder measurements; prefer a high gorge.
2. Use interfacing layered for lapel and roll stability; internal canvas and chest piece optimized for the extended overlap.
3. Provide a pattern pack with clear measurements: lapel, gorge, chest, shoulder pitch, length, sleeve head ease, and overlap distance from center front.
