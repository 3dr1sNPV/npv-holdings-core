# Business Summary — NPV Holdings (extracted)

Overview

NPV Holdings is a diversified holding company that combines high-end fashion (House EDRIS) with industrial engineering, aviation logistics, marine systems, HVAC/patent commercialization, audio production, and direct-to-consumer software and commerce. The organization emphasizes precision engineering, artifact-grade product design, and cross-disciplinary IP reuse.

Core Business Units

- House EDRIS (Fashion & Luxury)
  - Flagship: EDRIS Atelier / Tux Gen (bespoke tailoring; Tux Gen 001)
  - Brand assets: Masked Rabbit crest, fragrance line, digital/physical ecosystem

- Obsidian Motors (Automotive Engineering)
  - Restomod projects: Project Loki, Project Snow White 2.0 (Peugeot 206)

- Aero Logistics Systems (Aviation & Technology)
  - Civil aviation research, Electronic Baggage Tags (UHF/RAIN RFID, UWB)

- Abyss Marine (Marine & Fluids engineering)
  - Submersible buoyancy and pressure systems

- Kryon Thermal & Patents (HVAC & IP commercialization)
  - R&D, patents portfolio, thermal engineering

- Nocturne Audio Works (Music & Entertainment)
  - Production, studio infrastructure, audio hardware

- Kepler Technologies (Direct-to-Consumer & Software)
  - D2C retail (DaddysKiosk), e-commerce, software tooling

Strategic Themes & Strengths

- IP-first approach: patents and technical standards (e.g., HVAC, telemetry) are central assets intended to be licensed or spun into product lines.
- Cross-disciplinary reuse: mechanical and systems engineering inform product design (garment structure, hardware milling, telemetry).
- High-touch artisanal brand combined with heavy engineering credibility — differentiates offerings in both worlds.

Risks & Considerations

- Sensitive personal data present in the source export (Gemini export). Treat as private; avoid publishing raw contents.
- Execution complexity: running R&D and full product shipping across multiple heavy-technical verticals requires governance, funding, and focused teams.

Recommendations (high-level)

1. Formalize IP & corporate structure: move patents into Kryon Thermal with clear licensing pathways.
2. Create minimum viable products (MVPs) for each vertical before scaling (e.g., one EBT pilot, one Tux Gen capsule collection, one Obsidian demo car build).
3. Establish separated P&L and technical leadership for each subsidiary, with a shared R&D/IP hub.
4. Implement a data governance plan for personal / sensitive datasets found in the Gemini export.
