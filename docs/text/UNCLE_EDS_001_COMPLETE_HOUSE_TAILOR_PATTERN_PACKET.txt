UNCLE ED'S 001
ONE OF ONE — THE LEGACY TUXEDO
HOUSE IDEOLOGY · FIT · DESIGN · DRAFTING · CUTTING · CONSTRUCTION
This packet consolidates the design and measurement material retrieved from the user's prior tuxedo drafting work and aligns it with
the UNCLE ED'S house philosophy.
Pattern status: The pattern sheets in this packet are a drafting prototype for toile development. They are not a substitute for the tailor's
final block correction. Do not cut final 280–320 gsm wool until a toile is fitted and corrections are transferred.
HOUSE LAW
• The body is nature. The mind is civilization.
• UNCLE ED'S does not decorate the body; it civilizes natural form through proportion, construction, material and craft.
• Death completes the circle: the garment should feel capable of becoming an heirloom, an object that outlives its maker.
• The outside creates intrigue. The inside rewards curiosity.
• Nothing looks accidental. Exterior luxury; internal engineering.
• Cultural path: Mazandaran / Caspian nature → Tehran modernity → Paris → Rome → Los Angeles → New York. Influences are
translated into proportion, geometry, material and construction—not pasted motifs.
001 GARMENT IDENTITY
• UNCLE ED'S 001 — ONE OF ONE — 2026.
• Structured Boxy Hollywood fit: controlled mass, full chest, natural architectural shoulder, controlled waist, long clean line; never
sloppy oversized.
• Psychological target: composed, intelligent, cultivated, mysterious, subtly dangerous; never costume-like.
• Visible language: refined eveningwear. Hidden language: engineered tailoring.
JACKET
• Crossover single-button architecture; one functional aged-silver house-logo button, 25–28 mm.
• Peak lapel, exactly 12.0 cm finished width; high gorge; elongated sharp peak; completely straight outer edge; no bowed/scooped
line.
• Black satin facing, controlled sheen. White formal shirt and large black satin butterfly bow tie.
• Natural but architectural shoulder; high armhole; full canvas; hand-shaped chest; hand-padded lapel; precise sleeve pitch.
• Long double vents; clean jetted exterior pockets; concealed internal phone/passport/wallet/valuables/repair compartments.
• Four functional aged-silver sleeve buttons; 1.5–2 cm shirt cuff visible.
TROUSERS
• High-rise flat front; 4.5 cm finished waistband target, 5 cm maximum.
• Side adjusters, no belt loops; concealed hook-and-bar; jetted back pockets.
• Fuller thigh, controlled taper, minimal break; no visible satin side stripe in the definitive 001 design.
• Front rise 38 cm and back rise 40 cm are the later, more specific supplied values; verify on body/toile.
MATERIAL / INTERIOR
• Deepest black wool, approx. 280–320 g/m², low-to-medium controlled sheen, enough structure for architecture; subtle green depth
only if it reads black.
• Black/deep forest cupro/Bemberg lining with Persian geometric logic, Art Deco geometry, organic branching/circular pathways and
subtle asymmetry.
• Hidden oxblood accents. Aged-silver hardware. No military insignia, shoulder boards, tactical straps or historical uniform symbols.

RECONCILIATION NOTES
• The earlier drafting sheet recorded a 35 cm body rise and trouser targets of 103–105 waist, 116–119 seat, 64–66 thigh, 45–46 knee
and 40–42 leg opening. The later 001 packet supersedes the rise with front 38 / back 40 and supersedes the decorative satin side
stripe with no side stripe. Finished targets should be corrected during toile fitting.
• The earlier sheet proposed 11.4 cm (4.5 in) lapels. The definitive 001 design later increased this to 12.0 cm. Use 12.0 cm.
• The earlier sheet proposed 78–80 cm jacket length. The 73 cm figure is retained as a supplied reference, but final 001 length should
be selected during toile fitting for the long, clean silhouette.

DRAFTING FORMULAS & FINISHED TARGETS
JACKET FINISHED TARGETS — STARTING BLOCK
• Finished chest: 112–114 cm (105 body + approximately 7–9 cm ease).
• Finished waist: 106–108 cm (101 body + approximately 5–7 cm ease; do not cinch).
• Finished seat/hem: 116–118 cm (112 body + approximately 4–6 cm ease).
• Finished shoulder: 47–48 cm (46 body shoulder with controlled extension).
• Initial finished jacket length: 78–80 cm is the preferred long modern target from the earlier drafting sheet; verify against the supplied
73 cm reference and body proportions during toile.
• Sleeve: draft from shoulder→wrist 70 cm, shoulder→elbow 40 cm and elbow 35 cm; final cuff position must show 1.5–2 cm shirt cuff.
• Double vent length: approximately 26–28 cm starting point, then fit.
