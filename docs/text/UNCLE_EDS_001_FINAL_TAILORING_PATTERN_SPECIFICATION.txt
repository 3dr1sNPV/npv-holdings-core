6. DRAFTING CONTROL
Add seam allowances outside finished dimensions. Keep generous toile allowances at shoulder, side body, center back, collar, sleeve
and trouser seat/rise. Mark grainlines, notches, balance points, darts, pocket positions, button position, lapel roll/break, vent ends and cut
quantities. Every piece must read: UNCLE ED’S 001 / piece name / cut quantity / grainline / seam allowance.
7. FITTING ORDER
Correct in this order: front/back balance → shoulder slope → collar/neck → chest → lapel roll → waist/side body → sleeve pitch → sleeve
length → trouser rise/seat → thigh/knee/taper → hem/break. Never waist-suppress before balance is correct.
8. STATUS
FINAL DESIGN SPECIFICATION / BESPOKE MASTER DRAFT. This is the correct production brief and measurement control sheet. A
physical toile is still required before cutting the final cloth because posture, shoulder slope, arm pitch and left/right asymmetry cannot be
inferred reliably from circumference measurements alone.
