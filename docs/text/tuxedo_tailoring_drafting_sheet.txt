8. FITTING / TOILE CHECKLIST
• Make a half-canvas or inexpensive toile before cutting the final tuxedo cloth.
• Confirm jacket length on the wearer while standing naturally and while seated.
• Confirm the 4.5 in lapel remains balanced with the shoulder width; keep the peak long rather than widening it further.
• Check collar gap, lapel roll, front balance and button position from front and side.
• Check that the jacket skims the stomach rather than gripping it; remove excess fabric only after posture and balance are corrected.
• Set sleeve pitch and final sleeve length with the actual dress shirt.
• Fit trousers with the intended tuxedo shoes; set final inseam and break only then.
• Check that the trouser crease falls cleanly from the center of the thigh/knee to the shoe.
IMPORTANT: Target finished measurements are starting specifications, not guaranteed final pattern dimensions. A professional tailor
should draft using their own block/drafting system and correct the toile for posture, shoulder slope, arm pitch, balance and fabric behavior.
